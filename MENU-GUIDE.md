# Inventory, character and world map

Press **P** once to open the menu and again to close it. The tabs at the top are
**Inventory**, **Character** and **World Map**.

## Inventory and equipment

Choose your hands or a bag to see its contents. Drag wearable armor from the
item list onto a compatible equipment slot. Slots show their current occupancy
and capacity. Use the existing **Remove** action to take off equipped armor.
An incompatible drop leaves the item where it was. Equipping through these
slots requires a server running this update.

The ordinary item actions remain available. Equipping an item does not create a
new visible armor model; its appearance still depends on the original item's
model support.

## Character

The Character tab shows your stats and skill progress. Each weapon skill has
one base level and one experience bar. Spell schools and Parry remain separate.
This tab is the in-game character sheet; the character-selection screen still
handles creating or loading a character.

## World Map

The gold glow marks your current named region on the supplied Daragoth artwork.
Use **My Location** to center it, **Fit** to see the whole atlas, and the zoom and
scroll controls to read an area more closely. **Routes** shows map transitions.
Choosing another entry previews its information without moving your character
or changing the current-region glow.

Some small maps and interiors do not have a confirmed location on this artwork.
They remain in the map list and route view without a guessed marker. The atlas
is a world overview, so the glow is not an exact position inside a level.

## Updating an existing installation

Keep the previous release and your private character identity and save backups
before upgrading. Extract this ZIP completely into a writable folder. Use
**MSR-Launcher.exe** or **Play-MSR.cmd**; there is one current game.

See **START-HERE.md** for joining the two realms and hosting your own FN service,
and **Recovery/README.md** for using a preserved previous release.
