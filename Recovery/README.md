# Recovery

This ZIP contains one current MSR game. Keep a separate copy of your last working
release before replacing it. Your player-profile.json identifies your characters;
keep it private and backed up, and keep FN databases/save backups with their host.

To downgrade, stop your local host cleanly from Host controls, close the client,
and use your preserved previous release in its own folder. Restore matching
private profiles and host save backups deliberately. Do not run two local FN
hosts on the same ports or overwrite the only copy of your saves.

Legacy --version stable is retained for existing installations that separately
restore a complete Stable-Base and its recovery manifest/tools. A previous
runtime and executable recovery shortcuts are not bundled in this play ZIP.
