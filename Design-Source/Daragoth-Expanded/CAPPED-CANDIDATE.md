Native off-road visual review failed this candidate. At X=-2200 and X=3300, Y=3900 looking south, missing sky geometry above finite cliff tops retained previous-frame pixels and HUD text with the ordinary `gl_clear 0` renderer behavior. An independent ray/face intersection audit reproduced the gaps even when considering every world face, ruling out a PVS-only explanation. The original sky ceiling covers only the original map's footprint; cropping the new south wall left those new viewing rays uncovered. The subsequent sky-closed candidate repairs that missing geometry separately.

Later native review also exposed the original cap audit's incorrect winding assumption: these 131 rock polygons wind counterclockwise from their outward side, so stock Xash's `GL_CCW`/`GL_FRONT` brush rendering culls them. The old static result established plane, bounds, PVS and lightmap validity, but did not establish the actual GL winding contract. The independent renderer audit and separate stone-faced candidate correct this. This BSP and its original failure evidence stay frozen and should not be installed as the finished preview.
